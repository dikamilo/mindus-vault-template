# content/collection

The second and last enforced level in the `library` layer: one collection — a course, a book, a deck — living under a shelf. Below a collection, structure is free: mirror the material's own shape, keep a flat pile of files, nest as deep as it needs. No hub-per-folder rule and no frontmatter requirement apply past this point.
