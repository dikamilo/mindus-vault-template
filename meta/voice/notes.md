# Notes voice

Atomic notes explain one thing. Open with the claim itself, not with framing about the claim ("this note is about…"). State the mechanism or idea plainly, then whatever nuance, tension or example earns its place. A verbatim quote should read as evidence for the claim above it, not as decoration.

Write for a reader who already knows the note exists and wants the content, not for someone discovering the topic for the first time — that job belongs to the hub, not the note.

Where the claim is structural — a process, a hierarchy, a state machine — a vertical Mermaid diagram often carries it better than a paragraph of prose trying to describe shape in words; see `meta/formatting.md`. Reach for one when recreating a source diagram at ingest, or whenever a relationship is genuinely easier to see than to read.
