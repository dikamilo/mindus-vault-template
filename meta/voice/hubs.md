# Hubs voice

Orienting, not enumerating. A hub tells someone arriving at this folder what the domain is, where to start, and why one note matters more than another — never a bulleted inventory of what's inside. Write in the second person implicitly: "start with X" reads better than "the reader should start with X".

Name tensions and gaps honestly. "Unresolved: …" is a legitimate, useful sentence in a hub; a hub that never admits what it doesn't cover yet reads as more complete than it is. A `[!question]` or `[!warning]` callout is often the better home for this than a plain sentence — it separates "what this domain covers" from "what's still open" at a glance, which a hub especially benefits from. See `meta/formatting.md` for the syntax.
