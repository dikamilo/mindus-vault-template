# output/refactor

A proposal describing moves, splits, merges and renames with rationale, written by `refactor` to `outputs/refactor/` and kept indefinitely. `refactor` applies the change only once you approve it; approval and the resulting change are then logged, and any approved change touching a layer's top level updates that layer's `index.md` in the same pass.
